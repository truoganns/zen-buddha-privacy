Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Zen Buddha Wallpapers handles user information.

Information Collection and Use

Zen Buddha Wallpapers does not collect, store, or process personal data directly.

The app allows users to browse and set wallpapers on their device.

Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Device type and operating system
  • App interaction data

This information may be used to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

Data Sharing

The app does not share personal information with third parties except through advertising services like AdMob.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer.
